## CONTESTED-CLASS section — faithfulness-critical data

Per audit finding 4 (2026-08-07), the following exact numbers and counts are the **contested class** for the mechanics-only digitization. The operator has ruled that digitizing the mechanics is in scope; this section records precisely which data that decision covers.

### Terrain counts per side

- 2 arsenals
- 3 forts
- 1 mountain pass
- 9 mountains

### Deployment coordinates

- **Unit starting coordinates:** not fixed. The rules state free deployment within each side's own territory, one unit per square, planned without knowledge of the opponent's arrangement. Any "historical" or "default" opening position is therefore a player convenience, not a canonical rules value.
- **Terrain coordinates:** unverifiable from the acquired source set. The exact squares occupied by arsenals, forts, passes, and mountains are known only from the printed board diagram (and visually from RSG app screenshots), not from a coordinate table. Lines of Advance will draw an original board; the terrain counts and behavior above are the binding mechanical data.

### Why this class is operator-level

These numbers are the boundary between "idea" (the lines-of-communication wargame concept) and the particular mechanical expression chosen by Debord. They are the values that must be reproduced faithfully in code while every surrounding word, image, and brand remains original to this project.
