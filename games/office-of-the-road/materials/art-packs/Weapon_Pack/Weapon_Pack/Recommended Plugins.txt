To get a different graphic in RPG Maker MV/MZ for each weapon you need a plugin.

The ones I use is either Visustella Weapon Animation (MZ) or Yanfly Weapon Animation (Made for MV but can be used With FOSSIL plugin). 

Unfortunatley, I dont think either is free anymore. But I dont know of another Method to make this work.

You can try https://gabe-plugins.itch.io/akea-visual-weapons but I have never tried it myself so have no Clue how it Works.

Another invalueable plugin is CAE_WeaponSpritesAdjust, this just Works (for me at least)
