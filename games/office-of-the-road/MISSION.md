# THE OFFICE OF THE ROAD — mission

A fully streamlined JRPG roguelite: the party marches itself, and the player is the desk
it reports to. Everything a classic JRPG spends on busywork — walking, grinding, menuing
through filler — is automated; everything reclaimed is spent on agency: jobs (FF3-style
swappable trades), a tarot deck played into automated battles, routing, requisitions, and
the slow accumulation of certifications across expeditions.

Register: Candide, Don Quixote, Kafka. An earnest party in an absurd, indifferent world;
institutions that explain endlessly and resolve nothing; violence recorded as compliance.
The prose deflects — the instruments never lie.

For players who love JRPG builds but not JRPG time; for roguelite players who want a run
they can steer without driving. Success at maturity: a free web build whose 20-40 minute
expeditions read as one more filing at a desk you somehow care about — and a game whose
art (licensed packs) and score (code-composed) keep it paid-eligible if it earns it.

Non-negotiables: the six register laws; licensed-pack art with attribution; the band-kit
score; deterministic engine; the automation is the stage, never the player.
