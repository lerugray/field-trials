# ATTRIBUTION — THE OFFICE OF THE ROAD

This game uses licensed art packs. Credit is given below as their licences require. This file
ships with **every** build (DESIGN-SEED art law; CLAUDE.md hard rule #1). Full technical
inventory: `materials/ASSET-MANIFEST.md`.

No art in this game is AI-generated, downloaded ad hoc, or code-drawn in place of pack art.
All visual assets come from the licensed packs credited here. The score is code-composed
WebAudio (no audio files); it requires no third-party attribution.

restricted-licence note.)

## Score

Music and sound are **code-composed WebAudio** (the Chapel Perilous band kit, `src/band.js`),
generated at runtime with zero audio files. No third-party audio attribution is required.
