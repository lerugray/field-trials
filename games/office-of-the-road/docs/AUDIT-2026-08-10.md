# Verdict

**BUILT-WITH-GAPS**, not fully built and not substantially incomplete.

The playable core is real: deterministic march, job swapping, auto-combat, tarot interventions, equipment economy, routing, mastery, reports, licensed art, and five score states all exist. The green 145-test suite and six gates are legitimate within their narrow scopes.

Material gaps are:

- Broken/incomplete exact-resume and closed-run persistence.
- An acceptance soak that does not perform the claimed reload or per-expedition real path.
- Inaccurate card threat instrumentation and inadequate intervention probes.
- Side-mandates, mandate-node cadence, successful expedition closure, shrines, job/route unlocks, and genuine deck slots absent.
- M0 transition/cross-equip depth absent.
- Orientation reduced to pre-checked text.
- “Full art pass” extremely sparse, especially bestiary/town/battle motion.
- Attribution not packaged with the single-file build.
- Forbidden Character Creator originals tracked and pushed.
- Score not auditioned by the operator.

The three highest-value next build increments are:

1. **Persistence and acceptance integrity:** serialize live combat/hand/draft/report/progress state, atomically close runs, prevent double banking, and replace the soak with an actual browser quit/reload that verifies mutations after every verb through the normal animation/report path.
2. **Complete the agency spine:** exact threat targeting, pause on every relevant draw, real duplicate-input tests, side-mandate acceptance, mandate-node drafts/successful return, and genuine job/route/deck-capacity clearances.
3. **Monster Pack bestiary pass:** terrain/tier-specific monsters, distinct enemy verbs, animation frames, and a restrained town vignette.

I did not human-play the game, audition the score, perform manual mouse-only/keyboard-only passes, boot the HTML in Chrome, run the mutating build/soak scripts, inspect every PNG visually, verify the remote repository’s visibility, or independently validate the paid Pixel Tarot licence. I did run `node --test` and `node scripts/gates.mjs`; both passed. The repository was not modified.
