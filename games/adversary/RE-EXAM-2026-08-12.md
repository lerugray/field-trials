## 4. The ending — FIXED (dead end closed), with a new defect

Reached by driving the game's own `toNextStage()` path to completion (`mode === 'campaign-clear'`,
`campaign.index === 6`).

**Legibility — fixed.** Panel interior holds two colours:

| Element | Colour | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| `CAMPAIGN CLEAR` | `#f0c84a` | **11.30:1** (audit: 2.14:1) |
| `THANKS FOR PLAYING` / `PRESS K TO START A NEW RUN` | `#e6dcbf` | **13.28:1** |

The gameplay HUD is gone — `bottomHudModel('campaign-clear', stage)` returns `[]` and `render()`
skips it. Capture `f4-campaign-clear.png`. The frame is genuinely stopped where it matters: over
1.2 s, **0** pixels changed inside the panel band; 542 changed below it, all ambient ground
dressing across 39 columns.

**The exit — fixed.** With real key events, one at a time:

- `Escape` → stays in `campaign-clear` (correct; the handler takes JUMP/CONFIRM/ATTACK only)
- `KeyJ` → `play`
- `KeyK` → `play`
- `Space` → `play`

A `window` sentinel set before the test **survived every case**, so no page reload occurs. The
audit's dead end is genuinely closed.

**New defect — the restart does not restart.** `PRESS K TO START A NEW RUN` is false for anyone
who actually played to the end. `restartRun()` resets `campaign.index = 0`, `taken = []`,
`choice = null`, then calls `loadRun()` — which re-reads the autosave and does
`campaign.index = save.campaignIndex; campaign.taken = save.taken; campaign.choice = save.choice`,
overwriting the reset it just performed.

Measured on a genuine finisher's `localStorage` (`campaignIndex: 5`, `taken: ["s1","s2","s3l","s4","s5l"]`):

| | before `K` | after `K` |
|---|---|---|
| mode | `campaign-clear` | `play` |
| `campaign.index` | 6 | **5** |
| `campaign.taken` | s1…s6 | **s1…s5l (restored from save)** |

The player lands in the **Stage 6 keep**, not Stage 1 — visible in `f4c-after-K-realsave.png`
(red-lit keep, armoured spear enemies) against the blue cemetery of `f1-cold-open.png`. Gear and
level reset, campaign position does not. With `localStorage` cleared the same press yields
`index 0`, which is why this tests clean and why the lane did not catch it.

Not a crash and far milder than the original dead end — but it is a false statement on the payoff
screen, shown to every stranger who finishes.

## Register glance — the new opaque scrim panels

They sit inside the game's dark-stone look; they do not read as bolted-on gray boxes. The scrim is
near-black `#14151e` — *darker* than the world behind it — set inside the same chunky carved bevel
the ratified bottom HUD plate and the HP/GLD frames already use. On screen it reads as a slot
recessed into stone, not a UI card floating over the art. The pause panel, the action menu, the
clear panel and the bottom bar are now visibly one family, which they were not before.

Two honest notes. The interiors are **flat and untextured** where the surrounding frames are
dithered stone — a deliberate legibility trade (opaque means terrain luminance cannot leak into the
text contrast), but it does read slightly more "inkwell" than "carved slab" up close. And on the
pause screen the two art-credit lines fall **outside** the panel onto the raw dimmed world
(`y=218`/`230` vs a panel ending at `y=206`), so they float below the slab rather than sitting in
it; they are legible (white, ≥17:1) but compositionally orphaned.

## Verdict — **NOT CLEAR TO RELEASE**

All five FIX-BEFORE-SHIP findings are genuinely closed in the shipped artifact, verified on screen
rather than in source. The fix round did real work: the controls are taught, the pause and menu
screens went from unreadable to 13.28:1, the move list does its job, the ending responds, and the
QOL doc stopped lying.

It is not clear to release because two player-facing defects stand, both introduced or left by this
round, both mechanical, neither needing scope past the AR1 stop line:

1. **The fork screen is below the legibility floor** — 1.74:1 to 3.19:1, including the line naming
   the confirm key, on a screen every player hits twice. Same root cause as finding 2, fixed on two
   surfaces out of three. This is the finding-2 remediation being incomplete rather than a new
   problem, and it lands on strangers exactly where finding 2 said it would hurt.
2. **`PRESS K TO START A NEW RUN` does not start a new run** for any player with a save — it
   returns them to Stage 6. A false instruction on the ending screen.

Fixing (1) is a colour swap on `drawFork`/`drawProgressMap` to the same `PALETTE['j']`/`['4']` the
other overlays now use. Fixing (2) is ordering inside `restartRun()`. Both are re-measurable with
the probes in `docs/proofs/re-exam-20260812/`.

*No source, test, or build file was modified by this re-exam; nothing was committed.*
