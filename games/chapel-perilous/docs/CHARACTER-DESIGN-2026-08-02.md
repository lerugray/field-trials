## The thesis (one line)

**Verbs from stats, power from items, party from negotiation, magic from books —
and FNORD watching silently.**

Character power NEVER comes from the statline. Stats decide which options the world
shows you; items decide how hard you hit; your follower roster decides how much
force and strangeness you field; found books decide whether you're a caster. All
statlines are viable because a statline is a *conversation style*, not a power level.

## Stats — 4 visible + 1 hidden [all names are SEED; Ray's voice pass owns finals]

| Axis | Working name | Gates (verb families) |
|---|---|---|
| Composure vs the weird | **NERVE** | act-first, hold-the-line, stare-it-down, overawe |
| Hands + violence-competence | **CRAFT** | force, fix, sneak, impress-by-deed |
| Social gravity, conspiracy | **PULL** | bargain, recruit, talk-your-way, call-in-a-favor |
| Occult literacy | **GNOSIS** | read, ritual, bind, use-the-found-book |
| *(hidden)* | **FNORD** | never shown, never named in UI; rolled at creation; consulted ONLY by the unfair-tail encounter system (and possibly other rare hidden judgments — tail design owns it). The Law of Fives, playable. |

- **Pure verb-gating.** Stats never touch damage, accuracy, or HP. No stat check ever
  scales an output — a verb either appears (possibly colored by rank) or it doesn't.
- **Word-ranks, no numbers.** Each stat is a named grade, ~4 steps (e.g. SHAKY /
  STEADY / SHARP / UNCANNY — [SEED]). The character sheet has zero numerals for stats.

## Generation — deal a person, not a spreadsheet

Random creation like Cyclopean 2, **unlimited rerolls** (locked earlier, cp-003).
Each roll deals a complete stranger: a word-rank statline + a random **omen/quirk** +
one starting **oddment** (item). Rerolling should feel like flipping tarot cards
until you meet someone you like — identity-shopping, not min-maxing (there is no
"good roll"; stats are playstyle).

## Followers (the party spine — 1 PC + followers, locked cp-003)

- **Stat-keyed negotiation** (SMT-style, mid-encounter): the talk/stealth/flee layer
  the M2 combat design already anticipates. Your statline determines WHICH approaches
  exist against a given being — overawe (NERVE), bargain (PULL), bind (GNOSIS),
  impress (CRAFT). Different characters can recruit different beings; roster
  composition IS the build.
- **Wants/upkeep:** every follower has a small want (money, secrets, blood,
  attention…). Feeding it keeps them; neglect can make them **leave — or turn**.
- **Die AND defect:** follower death in combat is permanent; betrayal is real.
  The roster is alive, and very Illuminatus.
- **Capacity grows with level:** start 1–2 slots → ~5–6. Leveling = how much
  weirdness you can hold together; it never touches stats or damage.

## Death — permadeath, world persists

The PC dies for good (the unfair tails guarantee this happens). Roll a new stranger
into the SAME seeded world: map, cleared sites, and world history remain. Orphaned
followers may persist somewhere in the world — recruitable again, changed, or feral
(design hook for later; delicious).

## Magic — magic is loot

Spells live in found objects: grimoires, rites, blessed junk. **GNOSIS gates whether
you can use what you found** (rank-gated per book, not a spell list from leveling).
Casters are people who found the right books. Keeps item-driven power airtight;
build variety = what you found × who you bound.
