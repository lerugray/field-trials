## 1. THE M7a ART DIRECTION IS RATIFIED AT r3

M7b builds on the r3 staging exactly as shipped. Ratified, and not to be re-litigated by any
later lane:

- **The VACUUM SEALED stack as applied** (DESIGN-SEED §4.5): native-resolution software rendering
  into the 640x360 buffer, lighting as ramp-step selection, 8x8 Bayer dither and fbm for material
  grain, one curated palette in ten named ramps, the section composed as a single lit picture.
- **The desk as one picture:** an architectural section on the left, a sheet of aged manila on the
  right with the ledger written on it in ink. Overlays are documents laid on the desk, never
  dialog boxes.
- **The typeset ledger:** slab headings over double rules, label/value rows with leader dots,
  right-aligned figures, a reserved after-action report zone.
- **The type pairing:** **Not Jam Slab Serif 11** (display) with **Not Jam Serif 11** (body), CC0,
  embedded as base64 `@font-face`. This closes the type-register law (design codex 2.101) for this
  game: the register argument is on the record in the M7a r2 PROGRESS entry, both checks are met,
  and **the pick is now settled**. A later lane may extend the pair's usage; it may not re-pick the
  faces.
- **The legend zone and the title block printed on manila**, ink-on-paper polarity, per r3.

**The bottom status strip stays exactly as it is.** Ray was offered a change here and declined it.
Do not restyle, re-polarise, relocate, or "improve" the bottom status strip in M7b.

## 2. THE HUMAN CAST IS RATIFIED — the register is DRAB-VERSUS-ARMED

The M7a builder shipped an all-human cast from **NPC Pack — Human Empires** (Willibab /
Monsteretrope, CC BY 4.0) and surfaced the register consequence as the milestone's one big
design-axis question. **Ray has answered it: keep the human cast.**

**This game's register is officially DRAB-VERSUS-ARMED, not monster-versus-human.** The building's
own people are institutional personnel drawn from the facility's own materials and lit by its own
lamps; the raiders arrive lit differently and carrying arms. The comedy and the dread both come
from ordinary staff being processed by an incident, not from monsters being menaced by heroes.

### SUPERSEDED-AS-CAST: the seed's monster packs are DEAD for cast purposes

`DESIGN-SEED.md` §4.4 names monster packs as the staff cast source:

> Candidates confirmed on hand: `Dark-Fantasy-Enemies`, `Mythic-Monsters-I`/`II`,
> `Enemy_Galore_*` (staff) ...

**That language is SUPERSEDED AS CAST and is now dead text.** Binding, for every later lane:

- **`Dark-Fantasy-Enemies`, `Mythic-Monsters-I`, `Mythic-Monsters-II` and `Enemy_Galore_*` are NOT
  the cast source for this game and must not be introduced into it** — not as staff, not as
  raiders, not as officers, not as a variant set, not "for variety."
- They are additionally disqualified on technique: they are 64x64 at native scale against a
  cutaway cell of 14-26px, so using them requires downscaling pixel art, which violates
  §4.5 item 1 (native-resolution rendering) outright.
- **The whole cast is one pack, one scale, one idiom, one licence.** Any cast addition comes from
  `NPC Pack — Human Empires` or does not happen.
- Reopening this needs Ray's word, not a builder's judgement. A lane that reads the seed's monster
  language and acts on it is acting on superseded text.

Per `supersession-means-deletion`: this addendum is the tombstone, and the seed's monster-pack
cast line is to be read as struck wherever it appears.

## 3. M7b SCOPE STANDS AS THE SEED WROTE IT

M7b is **the full art pass plus the score** (DESIGN-SEED §9): the art pass extended from the one
PoC scene to every surface, the **House Band score** in the §10 register, collection-contract item
6 (one audio bus, unlocked on first gesture, honoured by `mute(bool)`), and an SFX pass in the same
register (stamps, drawer slides, distant structural failure).

**Cast discipline for M7b:** the cast is exactly the ratified human set. Do not add, replace, or
restyle cast figures beyond what the art pass genuinely requires. Lighting, placement and posture
are the art pass's business; identity is not.

### The score binds at three bars, not two

1. **House Band conventions** — code-composed WebAudio from the portable band-kit pattern. No audio
   files ship. Provenance stays clean and the game stays paid-eligible.
2. **The Song-Structure Law at the sharpened bar** (DESIGN-SEED §10): 3+ distinct sections,
   per-pass variation inside sections, section lengths measured in minutes rather than seconds,
   tempo slower than instinct. A single repeated cell is a defect, not a style.
3. **REAL HARMONIC MOVEMENT (new, binding — fleet lesson, 2026-08-14).** A sibling game failed
   Ray's ear the same day for being one chord with texture changes over the top. **Every section
   needs an actual chord progression with moving roots, and section boundaries need cadences.**
   Timbre variation, filter sweeps and density changes are not harmony and do not satisfy this.
   The listen set must be able to name the progression of each section.

**The score is gated on Ray's ear and is not closed by the builder.** A **LISTEN SET** is a
first-class deliverable, committed as soon as it exists: rendered audio files, at least two full
form cycles per context, loudness-normalized, with a `WHAT-TO-LISTEN-FOR.md` naming each section
and its harmony.
